About KELÒ

KELÒ is an independent artist exploring music through melody, emotion, rhythm, and unconventional creative ideas. His artistic direction draws inspiration from Haitian culture while blending contemporary sounds with a distinctive, dark aesthetic.

* Sounds: R&B, Afro-influenced music, dark melodic production
